# Where this simulator sits among the others

A feature comparison against the established C. elegans simulation projects,
and an honest account of what we have that they do not, what they have that
we do not, and which of those gaps are worth closing.

Written 2026-08-14. Claims about other projects are from their papers and
repositories, linked at the end; claims about this one are from the code and
the validation suite.

## The landscape

**OpenWorm** (c302 + Sibernetic) is the long-running integrative effort. c302
generates NeuroML network models at several levels of abstraction, from
integrate-and-fire up to multi-compartment Hodgkin-Huxley cells, and Sibernetic
is a C++/OpenCL PCISPH fluid-and-soft-body simulator that the network drives.
Its own overview paper notes the coupling is essentially one-way, neural
simulation to body mechanics, with sensory feedback still being incorporated,
and that runtimes are heavy: on the order of hours of compute per second of
animation. After more than a decade it has not reproduced the animal's
behavioural range, which the project documents openly.

**MetaWorm / BAAIWorm** (Nature Computational Science, December 2024) is the
current state of the art for closed-loop embodiment. A biophysically detailed,
multi-compartment neural network drives a biomechanical body in a 3D
environment, sensory neurons are excited by an attractant's concentration, and
the loop closes through muscle activation. It reproduces zigzag approach to an
attractor, and synthetic synaptic perturbations change the resulting behaviour.
Its stated limits: simplified sensory input, one behaviour (zigzag locomotion),
and a detailed network that does not yet span all 302 neurons.

**Boyle, Berri & Cohen 2012** is the neuromechanical reference model. Undulation
is generated by proprioceptive feedback rather than imposed, and the same model
reproduces the full swim-to-crawl transition and locomotion through
heterogeneous environments with no modulatory mechanism beyond the
proprioceptive response to the physical world. This remains the benchmark for
emergent gait.

**Izquierdo & Beer 2013 and successors** take the opposite approach: evolve
ensembles of small connectome-grounded circuits until they perform a behaviour,
then analyse the mechanism. Klinotaxis is the worked example, later extended to
klinotaxis plus random walk. These are circuit models, not whole animals.

**Kunert, Shlizerman & Kutz 2014** and **Wicks, Roehrig & Rankin 1996** are the
dynamical foundations most whole-network models use, including this one: graded
potentials, no spikes, synaptic activation as a sigmoid of presynaptic voltage.

## Feature comparison

Read "partial" as present but simplified, and "scripted" as present but
imposed rather than emergent.

## What this simulator has that the others do not

**Genetics as a first-class dimension.** The others simulate a wiring diagram.
This one loads the WBcel235 annotation, derives synaptic signs from CeNGEN
expression, and applies knockouts through the mechanism the gene actually
serves, so `cat-2` removes dopamine synthesis in eight cells and `dop-3`
removes one receptor's edges while leaving `dop-1` intact on the same cell. No
other project in this list can run a mutant.

**A life cycle.** Embryogenesis timing, four larval stages, dauer entry under
crowding and scarcity, a sperm-limited brood, senescence and death, with
lifespan responding to temperature and genotype. Nobody else models the animal
as something that ages.

**Extrasynaptic modulation.** Monoamines are wired by receptor rather than by
synapse, which is how they actually work; only 6% of those edges are also
chemical synapses. Basal slowing emerges from that layer, with the Sawin
dissociation intact across four genotypes.

**Validation as infrastructure.** 49 checks run the animal and compare against
published measurements, and the gaps are tracked as expected failures naming
the issue that owns them rather than being absent. Every parameter carries a
provenance tag that a check enforces. This is the difference between a model
that claims things and one that can be audited, and it is the single biggest
structural difference from the rest of the field.

## What the others have that this one does not

**Emergent undulation (Boyle 2012).** The most important gap. Our locomotor
wave is imposed as per-muscle currents; theirs falls out of proprioceptive
feedback and reproduces the swim-crawl transition as a consequence. This is
issue #10 and the honest headline limitation of this project.

**Detailed neuron biophysics (c302, BAAIWorm).** Ours are single-compartment
graded units. Theirs have morphology, compartments and ion channels. Our
network's activation range is compressed as a result (issue #17).

**Real physics (Sibernetic).** Fluid dynamics and a genuine soft body, against
our 2D viscoelastic rod at fixed drag. Gait adaptation to the medium is an
expected failure here for exactly this reason.

**3D.** Everything here is a plane.

**Populations.** One animal. Aggregation, social feeding and any assay whose
statistic is defined over a plate are out of reach.

## Missing features worth opening issues for

Ordered by value against effort, given what exists.

1. **Population assays: run N animals and score the plate.** Real chemotaxis,
   aggregation and dispersal indices are defined over dozens of animals. We
   compute them from one, which is why a chemotaxis claim had to be retracted
   after the scoring band turned out to be gameable by a blind walker. N
   independent instances is embarrassingly parallel and needs no new biology,
   and it would make several existing assays statistically honest.
2. **Egg laying.** A complete, well-characterised behaviour that is entirely
   absent: HSN and VC motor neurons, vulval muscles, the serotonergic drive,
   and the egg-laying-defective mutants (`egl-1`, `egl-6`, `unc-86`) that are
   among the most-studied phenotypes in the field. The peptide layer already
   carries `egl-6` edges with a signed receptor.
3. **The pharyngeal nervous system.** Feeding is currently a scalar pumping
   rate. The pharynx has its own 20-neuron nervous system, its own
   electrophysiology, and `eat` mutants with clean phenotypes. It is nearly a
   self-contained second animal and would make feeding mechanistic.
4. **Multi-animal social behaviour, once populations exist.** `npr-1`
   aggregation and bordering, the classic natural-variation result, needs both
   populations and sustained oxygen signalling.
5. **Proprioceptive CPG** (already issue #10), with Boyle 2012 as the target
   rather than a from-scratch design.
6. **Soft-body and medium coupling.** Not full PCISPH, but enough that drag is
   a property of the environment rather than a constant, which is what the
   swim-crawl expected failure needs.
7. **Male nervous system.** The connectome release includes the male; we build
   only the hermaphrodite. Mating circuitry is a large, well-mapped behaviour
   set nobody in this list simulates.

## Honest positioning

This is not the most physically detailed C. elegans simulator, and it does not
have the best locomotion model; Boyle 2012 solved emergent gait fourteen years
ago and we still impose the wave. What it has instead is breadth across levels
that the others do not attempt at all, genome to behaviour to lifespan, with an
audit trail that says which parts are measured, which are tuned, and which are
scripted stand-ins. For asking "what happens to this animal if I remove this
gene, kill this neuron, and run this assay", nothing else on the list is built
for the question.
