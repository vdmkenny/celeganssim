# Emergent locomotor rhythm: requirements

The locomotor rhythm is generated by an explicit ventral-cord oscillator
(`worm/body.py`). The network sets its amplitude, direction, frequency and
dorsoventral balance, but the oscillation itself is imposed. This note sets out
what an emergent version needs.

## Two candidate architectures

**Intrinsic cellular oscillators.** A-class motor neurons are intrinsic
oscillators, sufficient to drive backward locomotion with the premotor
interneurons removed, and the oscillation depends on the calcium channel UNC-2
(Gao et al. 2018 eLife 7:e29915). A Morris-Lecar implementation of this sits in
`worm/nervous_system.py` behind `NervousSystem.intrinsic`, disabled: no
parameter regime for `g_Ca`, `g_K`, their half-activations or `tau_w` produces a
physiological voltage swing.

**Proprioceptive coupling.** Each B-class motor neuron senses curvature of the
region just anterior to it, so bending propagates down the body (Wen et al. 2012
Neuron 76:750). On this account the oscillation belongs to the neuromechanical
loop rather than to any cell, and the body must be in the loop for it to exist.

The second is the more promising route here, because it requires no cellular
conductances that have never been measured.

## Measured values

Resting potentials, whole-cell current clamp in situ (Liu, Chen & Wang 2014 Nat
Commun 5:5155, Table 1). VA5, VB6 and VD5 are the only ventral cord motor
neurons ever patched; DA, DB, DD and AS have never been recorded.

| Cell | Resting potential |
|---|---|
| VA5 | −71.7 ± 2.4 mV (n = 15) |
| VB6 | −53.2 ± 2.5 mV (n = 13) |
| VD5 | −45.8 ± 1.9 mV (n = 15) |

A and B class sit 19 mV apart and are not interchangeable.

The +120 mV calcium reversal in the textbooks does not apply to this animal. It
appears in the C. elegans literature only as a fitted parameter in an AWA model
(Liu et al. 2018 Table S4). At −40 mV it overstates the driving force by 1.7x,
and a regenerative conductance amplifies that into runaway depolarisation.

## Conductances

**Inward.** EGL-19 is the only calcium conductance with a measured I-V:
activation threshold −30 mV, V_0.5 +0.6 mV transient and +6.4 mV maintained,
k = 4.7 / 7.9 mV, activation tau 0.5-1.0 ms (Jospin et al. 2002). In a neuron
its activation shifts 20-40 mV negative. UNC-2 is the rhythm generator on
genetic grounds (Gao et al. 2018; Jiang et al. 2022) but its conductance and
gating have never been measured in a worm neuron and are free parameters.

CCA-1 and NCA can be left out of VA/VB entirely: `cca-1(ad1650)` and
`nca-1; nca-2` double nulls have normal whole-cell currents in VA5, VB6 and VD5.

**Outward.** SLO-2 dominates in A and B class, and carries a hard validation
target: deleting it must depolarise VA by 25 mV (−71.7 to −46.5) and VB by
29 mV (−53.2 to −24.0), and must remove 80% of the outward current at +70 mV in
VA5 and 67% in VB6. SHK-1 belongs only in D class (45% of VD5 current).

## Constraints worth knowing

**There is no measured cellular waveform for the gait.** The VA5 upstate lasts
14.9 ± 1.5 s and recurs one to three times a minute; body undulation is 0.5 to
2 Hz. No ventral cord motor neuron has ever been recorded in current clamp
during real or fictive locomotion. Reproducing the upstate is therefore not the
same as reproducing the rhythm.

**A single ohmic leak misrepresents the operating range.** The steady-state I-V
of ASER is flat from −90 to −30 mV, because I_Ca and I_K cancel across it
(Goodman et al. 1998).

## State of the field

No published model produces C. elegans locomotion emergently from the
connectome. OpenWorm's c302 forward-locomotion configuration uses 39 of 302
neurons and drives the wave directly: 406 of its 438 current injections go into
individual muscle cells on a `stim_num*800 + muscle_num*30 ms` schedule, and the
resulting ~28 ms inter-muscle phase lag is that schedule read back out. It also
discards the connectome synapse-count weights, and 457 of its parameters carry
the tag `BlindGuess` at certainty 0.1. Sibernetic couples one-directionally from
neurons to body with no proprioceptive return path, and its `-noc302` flag
produces the same crawling animation from a sine wave with no nervous system.

The oscillator here is labelled as such, uses every connectome weight, and lets
the network set direction and amplitude.
