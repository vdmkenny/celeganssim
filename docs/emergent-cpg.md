# Making the locomotor rhythm emergent

Status: **not done.** The rhythm is still generated by an explicit ventral-cord
oscillator whose amplitude, direction and frequency the network sets. This note
records what a first attempt got wrong, the measured parameters that should
replace the guessed ones, and the honest state of the field, so the next attempt
starts from data.

## What was tried

Motor neurons were given a Morris-Lecar pair of intrinsic currents: a fast
regenerative calcium conductance for the upstroke and a slow potassium
conductance for the downstroke, on the grounds that A-class motor neurons are
intrinsic oscillators sufficient to drive backward locomotion with the premotor
interneurons removed (Gao et al. 2018 eLife 7:e29915).

They oscillated. A parameter search over conductances, half-activations and the
slow time constant then found **no regime at all** with a physiological voltage
swing: every oscillating set swung 90 to 130 mV. The code is in
`worm/nervous_system.py` behind `NervousSystem.intrinsic`, disabled by default.

## Why it failed

**`E_Ca` = +120 mV was wrong, and it was the main cause.** That is the mammalian
textbook value, and in the C. elegans literature it appears only as a *fitted*
parameter in an AWA model (Liu et al. 2018 Cell 175:57, Table S4), not as a
measurement. Measured calcium reversal in this animal is far lower:

| Preparation | V_rev | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Body-wall muscle, 6 mM external Ca | +50 ± 2 and +59 ± 2 mV | Jospin et al. 2002 J Cell Biol 159:337 |
| ASER, 1 mM external Ca | +21 mV | Goodman et al. 1998 Neuron 20:763 |

At −40 mV, +120 mV gives a 160 mV driving force against roughly 95 mV for the
measured value. That 1.7x error in I_Ca is amplified by the positive feedback of
a regenerative conductance into runaway depolarisation.

**The leak was also wrong, by a factor of 25.** The inherited Kunert value of
0.01 nS implies a 150 GOhm input resistance. Measured input resistance in
identified C. elegans neurons is 2 to 8 GOhm (ASER, Goodman et al. 1998) and 1.6
to 2.2 GOhm (ASE, Shindou et al. 2019 Sci Rep 9:3430), giving g_leak of roughly
0.1 to 0.5 nS.

## Measured values to use instead

Resting potentials, whole-cell current clamp in situ (Liu, Chen & Wang 2014 Nat
Commun 5:5155, Table 1). These are the **only** ventral-cord motor neurons ever
patched:

| Cell | Resting potential |
|---|---|
| VA5 | −71.7 ± 2.4 mV (n = 15) |
| VB6 | −53.2 ± 2.5 mV (n = 13) |
| VD5 | −45.8 ± 1.9 mV (n = 15) |

A and B class sit 19 mV apart and are **not** interchangeable in a model. No
recording from any DA, DB, DD, AS or dorsal-cord motor neuron has ever been
published; any value for those is inference and should be labelled as such.

| Quantity | Value | Provenance |
|---|---|---|
| Voltage swing | **~33 mV**, −71.7 to about −38 mV, never overshooting 0 | VA5 upstate, Liu et al. 2014 Fig 2b |
| C_m | 1.5 pF (neurons generally < 4 pF) | Liu et al. 2018 Table S4; Goodman et al. 1998 |
| g_leak | 0.25 nS | AWA fit, Liu et al. 2018 Table S4 |
| E_leak | −65 mV | same |
| E_K | −80 to −84.5 mV | derived from Liu et al. 2014 solutions |
| E_Ca | **+60 mV ohmic**, or better, GHK with [Ca]o 2-5 mM and [Ca]i 50-100 nM | Jospin et al. 2002 |
| tau_m | 3 to 10 ms | derived |

Channels, with the useful detail that several plausible candidates are ruled out:

- **Inward:** use EGL-19, the only calcium conductance with a real I-V
  (activation threshold −30 mV, V_0.5 +0.6 mV transient / +6.4 mV maintained,
  k = 4.7 / 7.9 mV, tau 0.5-1.0 ms; Jospin et al. 2002). In a neuron shift
  activation 20-40 mV negative. Add UNC-2 as the rhythm generator on genetic
  grounds (Gao et al. 2018; Jiang et al. 2022) but declare its conductance and
  gating as free parameters, because nobody has measured them in a worm neuron.
  **Leave CCA-1 and NCA out**: `cca-1(ad1650)` and `nca-1; nca-2` double nulls
  have completely normal whole-cell currents in VA5/VB6/VD5.
- **Outward:** make SLO-2 dominant in A/B cells. It comes with a hard validation
  target: deleting it must depolarise VA by 25 mV (−71.7 to −46.5) and VB by
  29 mV (−53.2 to −24.0), and must remove 80% of the outward current at +70 mV
  in VA5 and 67% in VB6. Use SHK-1 only in D-class (45% of VD5 current).

## The caveat that matters most

The VA5 upstate is **not the undulatory rhythm**. It lasts 14.9 ± 1.5 s and
recurs one to three times a minute; body undulation is 0.5 to 2 Hz. **No one has
ever recorded a ventral cord motor neuron in current clamp during real or
fictive locomotion.** So there is no measured cellular waveform to fit an
oscillator to, and a model that reproduces the upstate is not thereby
reproducing the gait.

Related: Goodman et al. 1998 found the steady-state I-V of ASER is flat from −90
to −30 mV, because I_Ca and I_K cancel across the whole operating range. A single
ohmic leak term systematically misrepresents that.

## Where the field actually is

Worth knowing before treating the explicit oscillator here as unusually weak.
A survey that installed and ran the alternatives found:

- **c302** (OpenWorm) does not produce locomotion emergently either. Its
  `c302_FW` forward-locomotion configuration uses 39 of 302 neurons and drives
  the wave by hand: 406 of 438 current injections go directly into individual
  muscle cells on a `stim_num*800 + muscle_num*30 ms` schedule. The resulting
  ~28 ms inter-muscle phase lag is that timetable read back out. It also sets
  `conn_number_override` to discard every connectome synapse-count weight. 457
  parameters across its sets remain tagged `BlindGuess` at certainty 0.1.
- **Sibernetic** couples one-directionally from neurons to body, with no
  proprioceptive return path. Its `-noc302` flag produces the same crawling
  animation from a Python sine wave with no nervous system at all.
- OpenWorm's `master_openworm.py` prints "Not yet implemented" for both the
  movement-analysis and the fit-to-real-worm-video steps.

So the honest framing is not that this project fell short of a solved problem.
Nobody has closed this loop. The difference worth keeping is that the oscillator
here is labelled as such, uses every connectome weight, and lets the network set
direction and amplitude.

## The most promising route

Probably not intrinsic oscillators at all. Wen et al. 2012 (Neuron 76:750) argue
the wave propagates by **proprioceptive coupling**: each B-class neuron senses
curvature of the region just anterior to it, so bending propagates down the body.
On that account the oscillation is a property of the neuromechanical loop rather
than of any cell, and the body has to be in the loop for it to exist at all.
That architecture needs no unmeasured cellular conductances, which is exactly
the problem with the route attempted here.
